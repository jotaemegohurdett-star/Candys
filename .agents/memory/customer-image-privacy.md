---
name: Customer image privacy
description: Privacy rule for customer-submitted product photos that contain contact details.
---

Public product galleries must use a derived, sanitized copy whenever a customer photo contains a phone number or other personal contact detail. Keep the original out of rendered storefront imports and make the privacy treatment clear in the product presentation when useful.

**Why:** Product samples can contain real customer information even when the image is only intended to demonstrate the product.

**How to apply:** Before adding customer images to a gallery, inspect the full-resolution source, redact only the requested sensitive region, verify the rendered result visually, and import the sanitized asset instead of the source.
